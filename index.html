Calibration

Sixteen points will appear on the screen, one at a time, from left to right and top to bottom.

  1. Each point appears, then shrinks until it disappears.
  2. Look at the point until it is gone. The next one appears after that.
  3. Keep your head still and follow each point with your eyes only.
